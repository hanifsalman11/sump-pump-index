Frequently Asked Questions (Goulds Water Technology GSP0311 1/3 HP Stainless Sump)

How many GPH does the Goulds Water Technology GSP0311 1/3 HP Stainless Sump pump at 10 feet vs 15 feet of Total Dynamic Head?

At a 10-foot vertical lift it delivers 2,460 GPH (41.0 GPM). Once you add friction head from a check valve and two 90-degree PVC elbows (~14.5 feet Total Dynamic Head), real-world output is 2,140 GPH (35.7 GPM), with a maximum shut-off height of 23.0 feet.

How long will a 12V battery backup sump pump run during a power outage?

A 12V DC backup pump draws ~12 to 14 Amps while actively pumping. Paired with a 100Ah Deep-Cycle AGM or LiFePO4 battery, it provides 6.5 hours of non-stop pumping, which equals 32.5 hours (nearly 1.5 days) when cycling on for 30 seconds every 2.5 minutes (20% duty cycle).

Should I get a 1/3 HP or 1/2 HP sump pump for my basement?

For average water tables and vertical lifts under 8 feet, a 1/3 HP cast-iron pump (2,460 GPH class) is ideal. If your basement sits on clay soil, has a high water table, or pushes water up 10+ feet and out a long 25-foot buried yard pipe, upgrade to a 1/2 HP cast-iron pump.
